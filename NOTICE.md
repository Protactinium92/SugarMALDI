# Gum Composition Reference — User Guideline

> **Purpose**
> This guideline explains the carbohydrate composition and classification of commonly used natural gums, to help implement accurate queries using **SugarMALDI** software. The values are expressed as **approximate percentage composition** of monosaccharides and grouped sugar classes.

---

## 2. Gum Profiles and Recommended Interpretation

### 2.A. Arabinogalactan Gums

#### **Ghatti Gum**
- **Classification**: Arabinogalactan
- **Composition Profile**:
  - Pentose: 47%
  - Hexose: 37%
  - Deoxyhexose: 4%
  - Hexuronic acid: 11%
- **Typical Characteristics**:
  - Strong emulsification behavior
  - Good water solubility
  - Branched polysaccharide structure

#### **Gum Arabic**
- **Classification**: Arabinogalactan
- **Composition Profile**:
  - Pentose: 37%
  - Hexose: 45%
  - Deoxyhexose: 11%
  - Hexuronic acid: 7%
- **Typical Characteristics**:
  - Excellent emulsifier
  - Low viscosity
  - Highly branched molecular structure

#### **Tragacanth Gum**
- **Classification**: Arabinogalactan
- **Composition Profile**:
  - Pentose: 50%
  - Hexose: 23%
- **Typical Characteristics**:
  - Strong thickening properties
  - Complex molecular composition
  - Stable in acidic systems

#### **Cherry Gum**
- **Classification**: Arabinogalactan
- **Composition Profile**:
  - Pentose: 42%
  - Hexose: 37%
  - Hexuronic acid: 13%

#### **Peach Gum**
- **Classification**: Arabinogalactan
- **Composition Profile**:
  - Pentose: 40%
  - Hexose: 38%
  - Hexuronic acid: 14%

#### **Plum Gum**
- **Classification**: Arabinogalactan
- **Composition Profile**:
  - Pentose: 43%
  - Hexose: 36%
  - Hexuronic acid: 13%

#### **Almond Gum**
- **Classification**: Arabinogalactan
- **Composition Profile**:
  - Pentose: 58%
  - Hexose: 36%
  - Hexuronic acid: 6%
- **Typical Characteristics**:
  - Good water-binding capacity
  - Stable dispersion properties
  - Complex branching behavior

---

### 2.B. Galactomannan Gums

#### **Locust Bean Gum (LBG)**
- **Classification**: Galactomannan
- **Composition Profile**:
  - Pentose: 1%
  - Hexose: 99%
- **Typical Characteristics**:
  - High viscosity enhancer
  - Synergistic gel formation
  - Neutral polysaccharide behavior

#### **Guar Gum**
- **Classification**: Galactomannan
- **Composition Profile**:
  - Pentose: 2%
  - Hexose: 98%
- **Typical Characteristics**:
  - Rapid water hydration
  - High viscosity development
  - Excellent stabilizer performance

---

### 2.C. Rhamnogalacturonan Gums

#### **Karaya Gum**
- **Classification**: Rhamnogalacturonan
- **Composition Profile**:
  - Hexose: 64%
  - Deoxyhexose: 26%
  - Hexuronic acid: 4%
- **Typical Characteristics**:
  - High viscosity
  - Strong swelling capacity
  - Acidic hydrocolloid behavior

## 3. General Usage Recommendations

### By Gum Classification
 | **Classification**       | **Includes**                          | **Best Used For**                          |
 |--------------------------|---------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|
 | **Arabinogalactan**      | Ghatti, Arabic, Tragacanth, Cherry, Peach, Plum, Almond | Emulsification models, Branched polysaccharide studies, Water-soluble gum systems |
 | **Galactomannan**        | LBG, Guar                              | Thickening simulations, Viscosity prediction, Gel synergy analysis |
 | **Rhamnogalacturonan**   | Karaya                                 | Acidic polysaccharide systems, Swelling behavior studies, High-viscosity formulations |

---

## 4. Notes for Users
- Composition values are **approximate** and may vary depending on source and purification method.
- Use **gum classification** as the primary selection criterion when building formulation or prediction models.
- Pentose, hexose, deoxyhexose, and hexuronic acid values provide **grouped carbohydrate summaries** for advanced analysis.
